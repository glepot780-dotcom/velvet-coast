An open-world crime game in the spirit of GTA 6, built for [make.fun's Make GTA 6 bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-01) — $1,666 in USDC, top 100 paid.

**Velvet Coast** is a sun-soaked coastal city of your own: eight districts from The Strip to Sunset Pier, with traffic, pedestrians, a harbour, a beach and a police force that remembers you. You are **Rae Solano**, a courier driver who owes $12,000 to the wrong people. Three days. Twelve jobs. One way out.

## What is in the game

- **A complete open world** — explore Velvet Coast on foot and by car: 8 named districts, labelled buildings, parks, a beach and a harbour, all drawn procedurally in crisp 2D vector art with day/dusk/night lighting.
- **12 missions over 3 days** — deliveries against the clock, on-foot pickups, a tail job, a getaway, a casino vault run at four stars, and the five-star Vault Run finale. Each mission has its own story beat from fixer Marta Voss and the police radio.
- **Cars to steal and drive** — take any parked car (the police notice), or buy the Coral GT and the Sunset Phantom at Benny's Garage. Arcade driving with handbrake slides, damage and smoke.
- **Wanted level + police who respond** — 0 to 5 stars. Patrol cars chase and ram, roadblocks go up at four stars, and the heat only fades when you stay clean and out of sight. Pay Danner off at the garage if you have the cash.
- **Combat** — a pistol with limited ammo (on foot) and ramming (in a car). Wreck a pursuit car and it drops cash or ammo.
- **Economy + progression** — mission pay, a garage shop (armour, tuned engine, race tyres, repairs, ammo, bribes), four cars to own, unlocks across the three days.
- **HUD with minimap** — money, health, car health, wanted stars, mission banner with timer and distance, speedometer, radio dispatch, district labels and a live minimap with the mission marker, police dots and the garage.
- **Start screen, pause, save** — progress (money, missions, upgrades, cars) saves to localStorage and continues where you left it. Win by finishing all 12 missions; lose by getting busted or running out of time — both are written on screen.

## Controls

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| WASD / arrows | Move on foot, drive in a car |
| E | Enter / exit a car |
| Click | Shoot the pistol (on foot) |
| Shift | Sprint (on foot) |
| Space | Handbrake (in a car) |
| G | Benny's Garage (near the garage) |
| H | Horn |
| P / Esc | Pause |
| M | Mute |
| R | Retry a failed mission / play again |

## Play it

Serve `game/` with any static server and open `index.html` in desktop Chrome — no build step, no network requests, all sound is generated by WebAudio:

```sh
node serve.ts
# open http://localhost:8080
```

## How it is built

Plain static files under `game/`, no library, no build step. The source is split into files with one clear job each:

- `game/src/config.js` — tuning, palette, cars, districts
- `game/src/city.js` — the road grid, buildings, parks, beach, collision
- `game/src/missions.js` — the 12-mission story, radio lines, garage stock
- `game/src/traffic.js` — traffic cars and pedestrians
- `game/src/police.js` — wanted level, pursuit cars, roadblocks
- `game/src/world.js` — rules and state: driving, shooting, missions, economy, save
- `game/src/render.js` — the world painter (procedural vector art, lighting)
- `game/src/hud.js` — HUD, minimap and all screens
- `game/src/input.js` — keyboard and mouse
- `game/src/audio.js` — procedural WebAudio sound
- `game/src/shots.js` — the four staged screenshot scenes
- `game/src/main.js` — boot and the game loop

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) copies `game/` and `screenshots/` to `/output` and writes `/output/dossier.json` with the vendored make.fun library. The four screenshots in `screenshots/` are real renders of the game's own code, staged by `?shot=chase|mission|garage|dusk` with a fixed seed.
